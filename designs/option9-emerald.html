About Us

Rima Nayberg, Attorney at Law

Rima Nayberg earned her law degree from The Thomas M. Cooley Law School in 2009 and has been officially licensed to practice law in New York since 2011. Over the past decade and a half, she has built her practice on a simple principle: treating every client with honesty, empathy, and direct communication.

Honesty, empathy, and direct communication, with every client.

Her work hasn't gone unnoticed by the legal community. Rima was named to the Super Lawyers Rising Stars list from 2019 through 2021, a peer-nominated honor given to top local attorneys. She also holds a 4.8 out of 5.0 client rating on Avvo (with a 7.1 "Very Good" overall score).

What clients appreciate most isn't just the titles, but her practical approach. She takes the time to explain your options without the complicated legal jargon, keeps her fees fair and upfront, and fights genuinely for the best outcome in your case.

Practice Areas

How we can help

Landlord & Tenant

Legal representation and guidance for landlords and tenants in matters involving leases, rent disputes, evictions, housing issues, and other landlord–tenant concerns. We help clients understand their rights and responsibilities and work toward practical legal solutions.

  • Residential & commercial evictions (holdover & non-payment)
  • Rent arrears & rent disputes
  • Lease drafting & review
  • Housing Court representation
  • Repairs, habitability & housing issues

Bankruptcy

Legal guidance for individuals and businesses facing financial difficulties, debt, or bankruptcy. We assist clients in understanding their available options and navigating the bankruptcy process while protecting their legal rights and interests.

  • Chapter 7 bankruptcy
  • Chapter 13 bankruptcy
  • Stopping creditor calls & wage garnishments
  • Foreclosure defense options
  • Debt relief consultations

Real Estate

Legal services for residential and commercial real estate matters, including purchases, sales, leases, contracts, closings, and property-related disputes. We provide guidance throughout the transaction to help ensure that legal requirements and agreements are properly addressed.

  • Residential purchases & sales
  • Commercial transactions
  • Contract drafting & review
  • Closings & title issues
  • Property-related disputes

No Fault Collections

Legal representation for medical providers and healthcare practices seeking payment on unpaid or denied No-Fault (Personal Injury Protection) insurance claims arising from motor vehicle accidents. We pursue overdue claims through arbitration and litigation so providers are paid what they are owed.

  • Unpaid & denied No-Fault claims
  • No-Fault arbitration
  • Litigation in Civil Court
  • Claim review & follow-up
  • Representation for medical providers
